Terra rossa

A week in the American Southwest, from Route 66 to the Las Vegas sign. Sandstone, endless straight roads, grazing animals and far too many pre-dawn alarms.

Chapter 1April 5, 2026

Grand Canyon

We leave Las Vegas on Route 66, because of course we do, and by mid-afternoon we’re standing on the South Rim of the Grand Canyon. From the railing there’s nothing to give it scale; only the bands of rock make any sense of it. That’s where the trip’s first real jaw-drop happens.

Chapter 2April 6, 2026

Antelope Canyon and Horseshoe Bend

Two sides of the same sandstone: deep underground in the slot of Upper Antelope Canyon, then perched high above the Colorado River at Horseshoe Bend, right up to the evening’s last thunderstorm. Cue the Indiana Jones theme.

Chapter 3April 7, 2026

Monument Valley

US-163 crosses from Arizona into Utah and drops you straight into a western: cattle grazing, horses roaming free, and the great sandstone buttes still standing where everything around them has worn away. This is the sky Wile E. Coyote has been chasing the Road Runner under for 77 years.

Chapter 4April 7–8, 2026

Blue Mountains

A night in a dome outside Monticello under a sky thick with stars, then an early start, long lens at the ready for whoever lives in the juniper woods.

Chapter 5April 8, 2026

Arches

Over two thousand documented stone arches in a single park, with the snow-capped La Sal Mountains on the horizon. And that evening, the drive west turns out to be something words can’t quite cover.

Chapter 6April 9, 2026

Bryce Canyon

Up before dawn to watch the sun pour into the amphitheater, then a walk along the rim and a drive down the scenic road to the rock windows at the southern end.

Chapter 7April 9–10, 2026

The Ranch

Two nights on a ranch east of Zion: pastures, old-time wagons and a herd of bison happy to let you get close when the light turns soft.

Chapter 8April 10–11, 2026

Zion

Utah’s busiest national park, seen mostly through the eyes of its locals: chipmunks, bighorn sheep, deer in the brush. And the river that carved the whole thing.

Chapter 9April 11, 2026

Into Las Vegas

The last day is mostly road. Interstate 15 clips the northwest corner of Arizona, crosses into Nevada and runs right up to the most famous sign in Las Vegas.
