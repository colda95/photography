Con il muso all’insù

Nose up, literally: a small airfield on the edge of Turin with the Alps behind it, and the day Pratica di Mare celebrated a hundred years of the Italian Air Force.

Chapter 1January 2023 – May 2026

Aeritalia

A grass and asphalt airfield on the western edge of Turin, where the plain stops and the Alps begin. Light aircraft, helicopters and, one day in May, vintage warbirds and aerobatics.

Chapter 2June 16, 2023

Pratica di Mare

The Italian Air Force was founded in 1923, and for its hundredth birthday it filled the sky over its base south of Rome: historic aircraft, today's fighters, tankers and transports, and the Frecce Tricolori to finish.
