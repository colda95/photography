Casa mia

Home: the coast of southern Lazio, photographed over four years of afternoons. The Circeo promontory, the lakes behind the dunes, the sea at sunset and a village up in the hills.

Chapter 1December 2022 – August 2026

Circeo

A national park since 1934, and the place I keep coming back to: a limestone promontory rising from the sea, a forest that floods every spring, and coastal lakes where buffalo graze and flamingos spend the winter.

Chapter 2April – December 2025

My Rouen Cathedral

Monet painted the façade of Rouen Cathedral more than thirty times, at every hour and in every season. Mine is this view: the same lake, the same line of trees and the same mountains, seen again and again from the shore as the light keeps changing.

Chapter 3April 2025 – August 2026

The sea

Twenty kilometres of dunes and the same horizon, facing west. Every evening the sun drops into the sea, and on 12 August 2026 it went down already bitten by the moon.

Chapter 4August 20, 2026

Sermoneta

A medieval village on the first slopes of the Lepini mountains, looking down on the plain and the sea. One summer evening among narrow lanes, flowers on the windowsills and cats who own the place.
