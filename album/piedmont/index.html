Note piemontesi

Notes from Piedmont, through the seasons: the hill above Turin, the gardens and woods of the Savoy kings, a valley of golden larches and the high plateau of the Gran Paradiso.

Chapter 1January 2024 – October 2026

La Maddalena

The highest point of the hill that rises behind Turin. From up here the city is a grid in the haze, the Alps run along the whole horizon and, on clear evenings, Monviso stands alone to the south-west.

Chapter 2March 29, 2025

Venaria Reale

The gardens of the Savoy royal residence at the end of March, when the orchards burst into pink and white and the mountains behind still wear their snow.

Chapter 3October – November 2026

La Mandria

Once the hunting estate of the Savoy kings, now a walled park on the edge of the city: meadows, old oaks and a river, with deer and herons for anyone willing to wait.

Chapter 4November 1, 2025

Valle Argentera

A side valley high above Sauze di Cesana, at the moment the larches turn. The first snow on the ground, the stream running clear over the rocks and the whole slope on fire.

Chapter 5September 2024 – August 2025

Gran Paradiso

The Nivolet plateau, on the edge of Italy's oldest national park: high pastures, a stream winding across the meadow and peaks of grey and red rock.
